# 20EMA strategy — first pullback after a structure break

> Concept-level description. The detailed scoring rubric I use to grade each setup is kept
> private; see [Disclosure](#disclosure).

## Idea in one paragraph

Catch the move toward a target that a **broken** trend left behind. When an old trend's
structure is broken by a strong first wave, the first shallow pullback (to the 20EMA)
offers an entry for the next leg. The target is a high or low that the old trend
left unreached. In Elliott-wave terms, this aims for wave 3 after a wave 1 / wave 2 sequence.

## The sequence that must exist

1. **Structure on the left.** The previous trend has a clear sequence of highs and lows.
2. **First wave breaks it.** Price closes through the old trend's last higher-low or
   lower-high, which invalidates that structure.
3. **Second wave pulls back.** Price returns to touch the 20EMA without breaking the
   first wave's origin.
4. **Take the third wave.** Enter on the break of the touch candle; target a level the old
   trend left behind.

Every step must be answerable with a **specific price**. A step that can't be pinned to a
price counts as not met. That rule exists to stop "close enough" judgement when eager to enter.

## Rules

**STEP 0 — Higher-timeframe filter (pass/fail).** Same as the
[200EMA strategy](strategy-200ema.md#rules): the 200EMA on both reference timeframes must
slope in the trade direction.

**Entry**

1. 20EMA / 200EMA cross in the trade direction.
2. A wave on the left shows the opposite trend breaking down (a broken high/low structure).
3. A first wave has completed. Its extreme is in place and has not been exceeded as
   price pulls back.
4. The pullback touches the 20EMA.
5. Enter when a candle body closes beyond the high (long) or low (short) of the touch candle.

**Exits**

- **Target:** a swing high/low **inside the wave that confirmed the trend change** on the left.
- **Stop:** beyond the relevant higher-low / lower-high.

**Skip the trade if**

- no completed first wave can be identified;
- there is no broken trend structure on the left;
- no target level exists inside that structure;
- the candle right after the touch breaks the touch candle's extreme (entry too late, R:R degraded);
- higher-timeframe and execution-timeframe trends disagree.

## Disclosure

As with the 200EMA strategy, the individual ideas are well known. I grade each setup with a
fixed, multi-factor rubric that checks every link in the sequence above against market
data. That rubric is not published.
